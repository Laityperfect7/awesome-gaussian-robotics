# Code of Conduct

Gaussian Robotics Atlas depends on careful disagreement. Contributors may reach
different conclusions about a method's scope, code status, or evidence. We expect those
disagreements to improve the record rather than become personal.

## Expected conduct

When you participate here:

- discuss the submitted evidence and the catalog rule it affects;
- explain uncertainty instead of presenting an inference as fact;
- give contributors a reasonable chance to correct an honest mistake;
- disclose a relevant affiliation with a paper, repository, company, or product;
- respect names, pronouns, backgrounds, and levels of experience;
- keep review comments specific enough that someone can act on them.

Examples of unacceptable conduct include harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing private information, fabricated
evidence, and using reviews to promote or suppress a resource for personal gain.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments,
and project-managed community spaces. It also applies when someone represents this
project at an event or in another public forum.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate a conduct report in a public issue. Contact the repository maintainers
through a private method listed on their GitHub profiles. If that route is unsafe or the
report concerns a maintainer, use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools.

Maintainers will limit access to report details, ask only for information needed to
understand the incident, and consider both immediate safety and the pattern of behavior.
Possible responses include a private warning, removal of content, a temporary
participation limit, or a permanent ban. Knowingly false reports may receive the same
response.

Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is itself a violation.

## Editorial decisions are separate

Rejecting a resource because it falls outside the scope or lacks evidence is not a
conduct sanction. Likewise, a technically valid contribution does not excuse harmful
behavior. Maintainers will keep editorial and conduct decisions separate and explain the
applicable catalog rule when closing a contribution.
